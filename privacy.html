Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Your privacy matters — especially for health information. Here is exactly how INTNT handles data.

Everything stays on your device

Your profile, birth details, cycle logs, symptoms, moods, workouts, food logs, journal entries, routines and preferences are stored only on your device. We do not have accounts or servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

We do not sell, rent or share your data, we do not use advertising or tracking, and we do not use analytics.

Birthplace search

When you search for your birthplace, the city name you type is sent to your device’s built-in location service (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google on Android) to find its coordinates and time zone. That request is covered by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. We do not receive it.

On Android, the system requires location permission for this city lookup. We use it only to search for the city you type — we never read, track or store where you are, and we never use location in the background.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID; the app only asks Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us or any server. Notification text may appear on your lock screen; cycle reminders never mention your period.

Health information

Cycle, symptom, mood and workout logs are sensitive health information. Because they never leave your device, we do not collect, process or share them. If we ever add a feature that would send health information off your device, we will ask for your explicit consent first.

Sharing

If you tap Share (for example on an affirmation or grocery list), the content goes only where you choose to send it.

When you take a screenshot, the app may offer to share it. The image is created on your device and is only sent if you choose a destination; you can turn this prompt off in Settings.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete everything at any time in Profile & Settings → Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device (unless it was included in your own device backups).

Children

INTNT is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because everything stays on the device. Teens aged 13–17 should use the app with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Security

Your data is protected by your device’s own security, such as your passcode and Face ID. Keep your device secure and up to date.

Changes

If we ever change how data is handled — for example by adding accounts or sync — we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
